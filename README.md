### 🔭 What I work on
I'm interested in getting **evolutionary computation and multi-agent coordination out of pure simulation and into real autonomous systems**. My background is mathematics (ODEs, PDEs, Numerical Analysis, Mathematical Modeling, Wavelets Analysis, Advanced Mathematical Methods, Discrete Mathematics and Graph Theory), and I use it to ask why an optimizer behaves the way it does, not just whether it wins a benchmark.

- 🧬 **Evolutionary computation**: memetic algorithms and mutation operators for combinatorial optimization (TSP)
- 🤖 **Morphology–control co-design**: jointly evolving robot body and controller; heading toward heterogeneous multi-agent settings
- 🧮 **Mathematical modeling**: dynamical systems, mathematical biology, environmental/fisheries data

### 🎯 Next step
Applying to a master's in Japan (AY2027) to work on evolutionary computation for adaptive coordination of autonomous agents. After that, I'm aiming at applied R&D in logistics and fleet coordination, with a PhD kept open.
